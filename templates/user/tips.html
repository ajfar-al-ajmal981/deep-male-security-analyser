📌 Email Safety Tips

🚨 Phishing Awareness HIGH RISK
  • Never click suspicious links claiming urgency.
  • Check sender domain carefully (e.g., paypaI.com vs paypal.com).
  • Hover over links before clicking.
📎 Attachment Safety HIGH RISK
  • Avoid opening attachments from unknown senders.
  • Be cautious with .exe, .zip, .html, .js files.
  • Scan attachments before opening.
🔐 Account Protection MEDIUM
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
  • Use unique passwords for email accounts.
  • Never share OTPs or recovery codes.
🧠 Social Engineering MEDIUM
  • Attackers exploit fear, urgency, and authority.
  • Verify requests for money or credentials.
  • When in doubt, confirm via another channel.
✅ Best Practices LOW
  • Keep software and OS updated.
  • Report suspicious emails immediately.
  • Use DeepSecure scans before trusting emails.
